# Homi Shatam I - Electronics

HOMIS-I, ISRA's second rocket. Launched **11 March 2025** in Anand, Gujarat.

> Written on 2026-09-27 from the team's memory. No code, wiring or data from
> this mission survives, so this is the whole record.

## What flew

| Part | Role |
|---|---|
| Arduino Nano | Microcontroller |
| MPU6050 | Accelerometer + gyro |
| BMP180 | Barometer (altitude) |
| SD card module | On-board logging |

No telemetry radio.

**Ignition** was ground-side, the same as M001: an igniter on a long wire, fired
from a 12 V battery.

Not recorded: the firmware, the wiring, the battery, and whether the board
controlled parachute deployment.

## Outcome

The motor exploded within 1 s of launch and destroyed the avionics. Nothing
was recovered from the SD card.
